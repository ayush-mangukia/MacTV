# MacTV V2.0

21 October 2026 · build 2 on macOS and Android.

- Paired and encrypted Wi-Fi remote, automatic discovery and connection recovery.
- Movies and series library, cached artwork, fullscreen VLC playback, resume, subtitles and optional next-episode autoplay.
- Home dashboard with latest Continue Watching, Spotify artwork and transport controls, Freiburg hourly weather and rain radar.
- F1 and football shortcuts, in-app match/race reminders and football refresh, fullscreen, unmute and close-tab controls.
- Phone buttons, touchpad and keyboard, app-specific controls and overall Mac volume through supported outputs/MonitorControl.
- Signed standalone Mac app and minimal M/play adaptive Android icon with themed-icon support.

The release retains existing app identifiers, signing identities, pairing and local data. Protocol v1 is unchanged.
